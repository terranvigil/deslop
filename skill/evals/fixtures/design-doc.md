# Introduction

_Spokeline rebalancing proposal, draft for the ops review_

Rebalancing serves as the backbone of rider trust. In today's shared-mobility landscape, a dock that sits empty at 8am costs us more than one missed trip. This proposal delves into a comprehensive forecast model for the van crews. It's important to note that the current posture is purely reactive: dispatchers wait for a station to hit zero before sending a van, showcasing how little the trip data is used.

## Next Steps

The key takeaway from the six-week pilot at 40 stations is simple. Four changes follow from it:

- **Forecasting:** Predictive staging is what keeps the downtown docks stocked before the commuter peak.
- **Crew Routing:** By leveraging hourly trip history, we could trim van mileage in a robust way (~12% fewer kilometers, from 3,150 to 2,770 per week).
- **Dock Alerts:** The alert should not only fire at zero bikes but at a threshold that depends on the hour.
- **Budget:** Overtime is the pivotal line item here, and the crews could finish their loop before 6pm.

## Final Thoughts

Despite these challenges, the path forward is clear enough. Furthermore, industry reports suggest that operators leveraging demand forecasts see seamless, transformative gains in availability. Our own pilot underscores the point — a testament to the enduring value of planning a day ahead.
